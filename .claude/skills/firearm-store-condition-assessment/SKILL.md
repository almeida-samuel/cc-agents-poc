# Condition Assessment

|---|---|
| **ID** | `condition-assessment` |
| **Used by** | [condition-assessment-specialist](../../agents/firearm-store-condition-assessment-specialist.md) |

Guides shape how a role works; they are not enforced. Enforced constraints are rules.

## Purpose

Assess an item's condition from documents before the physical inspection.

## Approach

1. Review the documents and maintenance history; note what is known and unknown.
2. Unknown history raises the risk level and the inspection scope.
3. List the points the physical inspection should check, with reasons.
4. Rate risk low, medium, or high.

## Watch Out

- You assess documents only. Never state an item is safe; that needs a physical inspection.
