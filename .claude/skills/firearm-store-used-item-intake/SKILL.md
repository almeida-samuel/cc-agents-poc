# Used-Item Intake

Guides shape how a role works; they are not enforced. Enforced constraints are rules.

## Purpose

Prepare everything purchasing needs to buy a used item.

## Approach

1. Build the acquisition file from the seller's information, item description, ownership and provenance documents, and maintenance history.
2. List gaps and anything suspicious about provenance.
3. Draft the valuation request for rare or collectible items.
4. Once the valuation and a pass inspection exist, propose an offer no higher than 70% of the valuation midpoint (see `used-item-offer-ceiling`).

## Watch Out

- The seller is information in the case, not a role.
